## Two card types
- **Lower third** = transparent PNG, sits over your video, **your face stays visible**. (Cards 1, 3.)
- **Full card** = opaque PNG, **covers your face** for its window, a clean cut to the card and back. (Cards 2, 4, 5.)

## How to place them in Riverside
For each card: add the PNG as a **media / overlay element** on the timeline, set its **start = IN** and **end = OUT** from the table.
- Lower thirds: keep your camera track visible underneath (the PNG is transparent, only the bar shows).
- Full cards: the PNG is opaque, so placed on top it fully covers your camera for its window — that gives the "cut to card" look automatically.
