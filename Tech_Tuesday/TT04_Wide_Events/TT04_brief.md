## Six-Beat spine (from the candidate)
1. Problem-first: the unpredicted 3am question scattered across three tools. 2. Belief-line: the primitive should be one wide, structured event per unit of work, with the pillars derived from it. 3. Analogy: three pillars = three witnesses who each saw part; a wide event = the one witness who saw the whole thing and can be questioned after. 4. Named parts: the wide event; cardinality (power + cost); observability 2.0 (derive metrics/traces from stored events); query-after-the-fact. 5. Honest caveat: high-cardinality wide events are not free to store or query, tooling is maturing, the three pillars are NOT dead — for many teams still the right default.
