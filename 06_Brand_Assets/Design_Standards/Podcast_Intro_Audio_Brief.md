# Signal Drop Intro, Audio Sting Brief

**Purpose:** a short, recognisable audio sting for the Signal Drop video cold-open (`Podcast_Intro_Standard.md`, Option 1 waveform). Same sting every episode = instant recognition. Understated and premium, not a stock "whoosh". This brief is paste-ready for a composer, plus a DIY sourcing shortlist below.

**Note:** Growth/Claude cannot audition, license, or purchase a library track for you. The buy/license step is yours. **However, an original synthesised starter sting has been created from scratch (royalty-free, yours):** `podcast_intro_sting.wav` (~7s), `podcast_intro_sting_2s.wav` (logo-only), `podcast_intro_hit_stem.wav` (isolated hit). Use it as-is, or hand it to a composer as a precise reference to polish. It is synthesised (sine sweep + sub thump + pad), not a produced master, so a composer pass will lift the quality if you want it.

## Sync map (matches the visual timeline, 30fps, ~7s)
- **0.0-2.2s** Rising "signal" bed under the waveform: a clean tone or filtered synth that builds, low level, no melody. It should feel like a signal coming into focus.
- **~2.7-2.8s (frame ~81)** The hit: a soft, low thump / sub on the exact frame the title "Signal Drop" lands. This is the one moment that must sync.
- **2.8-6.0s** Short resonant tail under the held lockup; settle, do not swell.
- **6.0-7.0s** Clean fade to silence so it cuts cleanly into the episode.

## Tone and references
- **Mood:** precise, calm under pressure, modern B2B. Confident, not hyped.
- **Palette:** sine/triangle sweep, a sub thump on the hit, maybe one or two subtle digital "blip" artefacts, a soft pad tail. Minimal.
- **Avoid:** cinematic braams, big risers, DJ whooshes, anything busy or salesy.
- **Reference feel:** the quiet authority of a fintech/observability product launch sting, not a hype-y YouTuber intro.

## Technical spec (delivery)
- **Format:** WAV 48kHz / 24-bit (master) + MP3 320 for convenience.
- **Lengths:** a ~7s main cut; a 2s logo-only "stinger" (for quick opens); optional 4-5s Shorts cut. All share the same hit sound.
- **Loudness:** around -14 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1 dBTP (YouTube/Spotify friendly). Leave ~0.3s of head and tail silence.
- **Stems (ask for these):** (1) full mix, (2) the title-hit on its own stem, so the editor can nudge sync to frame 81 without re-rendering.
- **Channels:** stereo.
