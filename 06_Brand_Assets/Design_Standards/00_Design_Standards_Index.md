## Two-surface model (2026-07-03)
MO uses two palettes sharing DM Sans + Space Mono: the **web/email surface** (Montserrat + teal #2F9E8D, light) and the **dark visual-asset surface** (navy #0a0e17 + mint #64ffda + Archivo Black; §24.11, Diagram Standard, thumbnails, OG, social). Semantic tokens (dark-asset): amber #ffd166 = high/caution, **green #7bd88f = low/safe (ratified 2026-07-03)**. Full note in `Brand_Design_System_v2.md`.

## Shared brand tokens (all assets)

- **Palette:** navy `#0a0e17` (with `#0c1929` / `#0e1f35` gradient), deep teal `#0d7377`, mid `#14a3a8`, mint `#2dd4bf`, bright mint `#64ffda`, amber `#ffd166` (emphasis only), white ink, grey `#9fb0bd` (dark) / `#6c7a82` (light backgrounds).
- **Typography:** Archivo Black (display), DM Sans (body), Space Mono (labels). Decks map to Arial Black / Calibri / Consolas for portability.
- **Motif:** the lens and crosshair from the logo. Subtle grid pattern and mint glow on dark canvases.
- **Voice on visuals:** UK English, no em dashes, no AI tells, one insight per visual, confirmed figures only.

## Running the templates

`templates/mo_visual_kit.js` (Node, needs sharp): run with a command and arguments, for example
`node mo_visual_kit.js blogthumb --title "..." --sub "..." --eyebrow "THE OBSERVABILITY DIGEST" --out thumb.png`.
Commands: `blogthumb`, `ytthumb` (`--headshot path` for the portrait), `bookends` (`--slug`, `--title`, `--sub`, `--marker`, `--badge`), `diagram-sample`. Reference renders for every asset are in `samples/`.

## QA gates (apply to everything)

Open the rendered file and look at it before shipping, at full size and at thumbnail size. The Codex mandatory visual QA gate (§19.6) applies in spirit to all assets: rendering is not shipping. Check nothing is cropped, text is legible small, contrast holds, and the asset is recognisably the same brand as the samples in this folder.
