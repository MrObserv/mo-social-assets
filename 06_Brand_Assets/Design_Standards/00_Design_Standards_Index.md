## One palette, two modes (v3, 2026-09-11 — supersedes the three-surface model)

v3 collapsed three surfaces into **one palette with a light and a dark mode**. **Writing renders light:** a blog, article, newsletter or any card fronting written material takes the website palette, because it is the same reading surface. Everything else — podcast, YouTube, episode art, short-form, feed cards for non-writing assets — renders dark.

Mode is a property of the **surface**, not a command-line choice: it is resolved by `modeFor()` / `mode_for()`, and only the surfaces declared in `mode.dual_mode` accept a requested mode at all. Asking for dark on a writing surface raises.

The three-surface model (web/email, dark visual-asset, white architecture) is **RETIRED**. Full note in `Brand_Design_System_v3.md`; `Brand_Design_System_v2.md` is historical and must not be used as a reference.

## Shared brand tokens (all assets)

**No hex is written here, deliberately.** A value in two places drifts, and this section quoted the entire retired v2 palette as current until 2026-09-18. For any value, read `design-tokens.json` or run `node producers/mo_visual_kit.js tokens`.

- **Palette, by token name:** light is ground, panel, tint, ink, navy, muted, soft, teal, teal-deep, border, hairline. Dark is dark-ground, dark-panel, dark-motif, dark-border, teal, teal-bright, on-dark-body, on-dark-soft. **Semantic pairs are mode-dependent** — the same meaning is a different value in each mode, so a dark semantic on a light ground is a fail.
- **Typography:** **Montserrat ExtraBold** (display), DM Sans (body), Space Mono (labels and kickers, on every surface including architecture diagrams). **Archivo Black is NOT a brand face.** It was named here and in a producer, resolved to nothing, fell back silently, and produced a blank card.
- **Marks:** the **ring mark** is the identifier, resolved by NAME from the marks manifest and never by filename. The crosshair is furniture and never identifies the brand on its own. The lens / eye mark is **retired**, and the two logo files carrying it are that retired mark, not a fallback.
- **Canvas:** a flat ground plus **ONE** radial teal wash, opacity at `structure.wash` (dark and light values both live there). The gradient, the grid and the glow are retired as **concepts**, not merely as values.
- **Voice on visuals:** UK English, no em dashes, no AI tells, one insight per visual, confirmed figures only.

## Running the templates

`templates/mo_visual_kit.js` (Node, needs sharp): run with a command and arguments, for example
`node mo_visual_kit.js blogthumb --title "..." --sub "..." --eyebrow "THE OBSERVABILITY DIGEST" --out thumb.png`.
Commands: `blogthumb`, `ytthumb` (`--headshot path` for the portrait), `bookends` (`--slug`, `--title`, `--sub`, `--marker`, `--badge`), `diagram-sample`. Reference renders for every asset are in `samples/`.

## QA gates (apply to everything)

Open the rendered file and look at it before shipping, at full size and at thumbnail size. The Codex mandatory visual QA gate (§19.6) applies in spirit to all assets: rendering is not shipping. Check nothing is cropped, text is legible small, contrast holds, and the asset is recognisably the same brand as the samples in this folder.
