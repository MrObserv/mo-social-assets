# Thumbnail Producer Routing

**Created:** 2026-08-21 · **Corrected:** 2026-08-24 (Control) · **Owner:** Control (code) / Growth (design lanes) · **Purpose:** answer "which producer do I use?" in one screen.

Five producers currently render thumbnail-family and card art. They are not interchangeable. Pick by **series and surface**, not by whichever script you opened last.

## READ THIS FIRST: EPISODE OG vs BLOG OG

Both are 1200x630. They are **different cards from different producers**, and confusing them is what caused the misroute on **2026-08-24**.

| You need a 1200x630 card for... | Producer | Why the other one is wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A **Signal Drop EPISODE** (podcast OG / episode newsletter card) | `thumbnail_builder.py` `compose_og()` | It is episode-shaped by design: the right column is the hardcoded `ALLAN'S HARD STOP` label and the footer is `EPISODE {n} • ALLAN MANN`. |
| A **BLOG post or a BYTE-SIZE** (post OG, also the social-share preview) | `mo_visual_kit.js blogthumb` | `compose_og()` would stamp a blog with `ALLAN'S HARD STOP` and an episode-number footer. It is not a blog card and must never be used as one. |
| **The Signal** weekly newsletter masthead | `thumbnail_builder.py` `compose_signal()` / `--signal-only` | Its own card standard: MASTERING OBSERVABILITY house lockup, no headshot, issue counter. Never a reused blog OG or episode OG. |

## Routing in one line each

- **Signal Drop episode asset?** → `thumbnail_builder.py` v1.4.0. It is **CANONICAL** for the EPISODE OG card, square episode art and the 9:16 bookends. It is called automatically by `00_Command_Center/episode_asset_watcher_v5.py`, and it is registered in `00_Command_Center/Canonical_Registry_2026-08-06.md` (the registry also names the sandbox copy as the thing that must be synced before every render).
- **The Signal weekly newsletter masthead card?** → `thumbnail_builder.py` v1.4.0 with **`--signal-only`** (`compose_signal()`, added at v1.4.0 on 2026-08-21 under GR-2026-08-21-02). `--title` is the week's lead headline, `--episode` is the continuing issue number (the 2026-08-21 rebrand is ISSUE 101, never "01"), `--date` is the display date; `--subtitle` is not required for this surface. Governed by `The_Signal_Card_Standard.md`, built weekly by the `the-signal-weekly-newsletter` Thursday task.
- **Blog post or byte-size OG card (1200x630)?** → **`node templates/mo_visual_kit.js blogthumb`**, NOT `thumbnail_builder.py`. Full line and the mandatory `--out` location are in `Blog_Thumbnail_Standard.md`: `node templates/mo_visual_kit.js blogthumb --title "..." --sub "..." --eyebrow "..." --out 04_Newsletter_and_Blog/<date>_<slug>/<slug>_og_1200x630.png`. `thumbnail_builder.py`'s `compose_og()` is the EPISODE card and hardcodes `ALLAN'S HARD STOP` plus an `EPISODE {n} • ALLAN MANN` footer, so it is wrong for a blog.
- **In-body or standalone diagram?** → `templates/mo_diagram.js`. `--theme light` for in-body blog and email, `--theme dark` (the default) for standalone assets. Never hand-roll a one-off light producer.
- **Tech Tuesday surface?** → `tt_thumbnail_builder.py`. It is the Tech Tuesday surfaces producer: titlecard 1920x1080, Spotify square 1500, YouTube 1280x720, plus the QA contact sheet. The watcher auto-invokes it when a TT headshot lands.
- **Hero / vidIQ-grade face thumbnail?** → **do not use in production.** `hero_thumbnail.py` is a **PARKED POC** (Al, 2026-08-21): the build is parked pending either a full thumbnail-skill design or a deliberate decision to use stronger external AI image models. Its division of labour is that Gemini (or any matting tool) owns the **headshot cut-out** and the producer owns **everything else** (mint-gradient title stack, terminal window, deterministic node/edge diagram from `--diagram` JSON, branding bar + day chip, navy/mint background, rim glow). Layouts: **B (explainer)** when the episode has a real technical object to show (three-tier title, terminal window, node diagram, headshot right), **A (simple)** when it does not (branding bar + badge, two-tier title, ghosted chart, headshot right); layout is inferred from `--diagram` unless `--layout` is passed.

## Rules (all producers in this table)

1. **Sync the sandbox copy from the canonical before rendering.** This is the producer-lock discipline in `OG_Card_Standard.md`: in any sandbox session, copy the canonical G: producer over the sandbox copy FIRST and confirm they match (`BUILDER_VERSION` plus line count). The two Node producers carry no version constant, so match them on line count and byte size instead. A stale sandbox copy is what doubled the OG footer domain and dropped the vertical divider on Ep 26.
2. **Fonts come from `mo-social-assets/fonts`, never the G: stream.** Point `MO_FONTS` at the `mo-social-assets` font folder; the Linux render sandbox cannot read the G: fonts. `MM_PROJECT_ROOT` points at a root holding `06_Brand_Assets/{fonts, logo-on-dark.svg}`. No producer ever falls back to system fonts.
3. **Never hand-build a replica producer.** No "preview" script, no re-implementation, no partial copy. Canonical producer only. A hand-rolled replica (`og_layoutb.py`) forced the OG quote into the wrong font and lost the spec, and together with the stale-copy failure it caused a full rework loop on **Ep 26** (2026-08-06).
4. **Rendering is not shipping.** Every producer above is behind the mandatory two-pass brand QA gate (pass 1 against the spec, pass 2 fresh eyes), at full size and at thumbnail size.

## Known inconsistency

The three-way split across the *thumbnail* producers (`thumbnail_builder.py`, `tt_thumbnail_builder.py`, `hero_thumbnail.py`) is the known inconsistency that the future **thumbnail skill** is meant to unify (**GR-2026-07-13-03**, which stays OPEN: the half-baked build is parked, but a full thumbnail-skill design, or a deliberate move to stronger external AI image models, is still required). The two Node producers (`mo_visual_kit.js`, `mo_diagram.js`) are a separate, stable lane and are not in scope for that unification.
